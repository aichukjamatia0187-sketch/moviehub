Privacy

Your privacy
matters.

MovieHub is designed to provide a simple movie and TV discovery experience while keeping the information we collect limited to what is necessary for the website to function.

Information We Collect

MovieHub does not require an account to browse movies and TV shows. We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, address or phone number through the website.

Movie Data

Movie information, posters, ratings and related content are provided through The Movie Database (TMDB) API.

Third-Party Services

MovieHub may link to third-party services when information about where a movie or TV show is legally available is provided. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies

MovieHub does not intentionally use cookies to identify individual users. Third-party services used by the website may use their own technologies according to their respective policies.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as MovieHub adds new features or services.
